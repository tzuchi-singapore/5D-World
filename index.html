如何游玩 · How to play

Phone: drag on the left half to walk, drag on the right half to look, pinch to zoom. 🗡️ = sword flight (▲▼ to rise/descend), 🏃 = run, ⤒ = jump.
Keyboard: WASD move · Shift run · Space jump / rise · C descend · F fly · E talk / interact · 1–5 activities · B build · G family · M travel · T time flow · drag mouse to look, wheel to zoom.
Slide the time bar at the top to go from dawn to midnight — stars, moon, lanterns and windows light up. ▶ / ⏩ / ⏸ sets how fast time flows.
Walk up to anyone and tap 💬 to talk, receive blessings or invite them into your family. Every area has its own living sound — forest birds, waves on the Jade Sea, the roaring falls, temple bells, crickets at night.
